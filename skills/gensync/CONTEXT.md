## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — score prospects against this
Strong fit (all/most true):
- **B2B** product (sells to businesses/developers/operators), not pure consumer.
- Product is **genuinely good but the story is scattered** — technical/complex, hard to explain simply.
- **Trigger present**: recently raised, about to launch, joined an accelerator batch, hiring GTM/marketing, or shipping a big release.
- Founder is **visible / willing to be on camera** (founder-led content is our edge).
- Startup stage: pre-seed → Series A (has some budget, still figuring out GTM).

Weak / skip:
- Pure consumer apps with no B2B angle, no founder visibility.
- Enterprises with a full in-house brand team and no launch trigger.
- Anything with no findable public contact AND no trigger (not worth the slot).
